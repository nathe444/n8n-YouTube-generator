# n8n YouTube Video Generator

This repository contains two [n8n](https://n8n.io/) workflow JSON files that automate the process of generating and publishing YouTube videos using AI-generated scripts, visuals, and Google Sheets for project management.

---

## Overview

This project enables you to:

- Automatically generate video scripts and scene prompts using AI.
- Render videos using the [json2video](https://json2video.com/) API.
- Track video production and publishing status in Google Sheets.
- Automatically upload finished videos to YouTube.

---

## Workflows

### 1. Video Generation Workflow

The [`generate video.json`](generate%20video.json) workflow orchestrates the process of:

- Fetching video ideas and prompts from a Google Sheet.
- Generating scene-by-scene voiceover scripts and image prompts using AI (Google Gemini).
- Sending the prompts and assets to json2video to render the video.
- Updating the Google Sheet with the video status and output link.

#### Workflow Diagram

![Video Generation Workflow](generate.png)

---

### 2. Video Publishing Workflow

The [`publish video.json`](publish%20video.json) workflow:

- Checks the Google Sheet for videos that have been created but not yet published.
- Downloads the finished video.
- Uploads the video to YouTube with the appropriate title and description.
- Updates the publishing status in the Google Sheet.
